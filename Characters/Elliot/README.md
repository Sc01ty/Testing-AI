# Elliot

The current art direction is the fresh game-style character in `V3`. Open `V3/Blender/Elliot_Game.blend`; portable exports are in `V3/Godot` and `V3/Unreal`.

V3 uses 2,576 triangles, a 17-bone rig, 59 named animation clips and 21 facial controls. Its geometry consists of a visible head, hands, neck and clothing. There is no anatomical body under the clothes.

The active Godot demo uses V3. The scene before the restart is preserved as `GodotTest/main_before_game_restart.tscn`. All realistic attempts remain in V2 and its History folder; the earliest model remains in Model.

All 121 audio files are unchanged. 18 representative recordings are connected to the reusable metadata-driven gallery in `V3/Performance/Reusable`; autonomous gameplay speech remains pending. See `V3/README.md` and `V3/VALIDATION.md` for the new direction, files and actual checks.


## Reusable performance milestone

`V3/Performance/Reusable` (or `Performance/Reusable` from V3) contains an 18-recording, 13-category performance gallery driven by 25 reusable primitives. Phone-to-ear actions are corrected. The standalone `GodotDemo/project.godot` and `Play_Elliot.cmd` provide interactive replay. The 121 originals and approved appearance are unchanged; Jev and autonomous speech wiring remain untouched.
