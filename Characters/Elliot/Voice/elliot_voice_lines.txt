=====================================================================
 ELLIOT — VOICE LINES (recording script)
=====================================================================

WHO ELLIOT IS (read this to your friend before recording)
---------------------------------------------------------
Early 20s. Ordinary British lad. Painfully awkward, polite to a fault.
He hates confrontation, hates eye contact, and REALLY hates people
standing too close. He apologises even when it's your fault.
He's not a fighter: when he's pushed, he goes quiet, walks off,
or gets his phone out and pretends to be busy.
He can't insult people properly. His comebacks are weak and he knows it.
He doesn't forget. He'll remember you, and he'll tell someone about you
rather than say it to your face.
He only properly snaps when you keep pushing, and even then
he sounds more scared than angry.

(This matches his config: social_confidence 0.18, personal_space_sensitivity
0.82, eye_contact_tolerance 1.25 s, baseline irritation 0.03.)

HOW TO READ
- Talk like a real person, not a voice actor. Mumbling is GOOD.
- Most lines are quiet. Only the lines marked LOUD should be loud.
- Read every line 2 or 3 times, a bit differently each time.
- Leave 1 second of silence between takes.
- Record WAV if you can, phone close to mouth, quiet room.
- Name files by the ID, e.g.  NOTICE_01.wav
- Lines marked * are the essentials. If time runs out, record those first.

HOW IT PLUGS IN
- The game already fires these events: OnPlayerNoticed, OnLongStare,
  OnPlayerTooClose, OnStepBack, OnPlayerFollowed,
  OnRepeatedBoundaryViolation, OnWarning, OnPlayerBackedOff, OnRecovered.
  The event bus picks a random variant from the group.
- Sections 11-17 are for when Jev is installed. Jev decides WHAT Elliot
  does (ignore / walk away / insult back / get scared / tell someone),
  and the line comes from that group.
- [clip] = the animation it pairs with.

 PART A — REACTIONS (works now, no Jev needed)

1. NOTICES YOU (OnPlayerNoticed)            [NOTICE_PLAYER / QUICK_GLANCE]
   *NOTICE_01   "Oh. Hey."                        (surprised, quiet)
   *NOTICE_02   "...Hi."                          (tiny wave energy)
    NOTICE_03   "Alright?"                        (polite, not inviting chat)
    NOTICE_04   "Oh— sorry, didn't see you there."
    NOTICE_05   "Hm."                             (just a noise, eyes down)

2. YOU'RE STARING (OnLongStare)             [BREAK_EYE_CONTACT / LOOK_AT_FLOOR]
   *STARE_01    "...Can I help you?"
   *STARE_02    "Is there something on my face?"  (touches face)  [TOUCH_FACE]
    STARE_03    "Okay. You're still looking."     (muttered)
    STARE_04    "Why are you looking at me like that?"
    STARE_05    "Right. Cool. Very normal."       (to himself)
    STARE_06    "Please stop staring, it's weird."

3. TOO CLOSE (OnPlayerTooClose)             [HAND_UP_BOUNDARY / CLOSED_POSTURE]
   *CLOSE_01    "Bit close, mate."
   *CLOSE_02    "Could you just— back up a bit?"
    CLOSE_03    "Personal space, yeah?"
    CLOSE_04    "Woah— okay, hi, hello."          (startled)
    CLOSE_05    "You're, um... really close."

4. HE STEPS BACK (OnStepBack)               [STEP_BACK / BACKPEDAL_SHORT]
   *STEPBACK_01 "Sorry, I just—"                  (trails off)
    STEPBACK_02 "I'll just... go here."
    STEPBACK_03 "Nope. Okay."                     (under breath)
    STEPBACK_04 (nervous laugh only, 1-2 sec)

5. YOU FOLLOWED HIM (OnPlayerFollowed)      [STOP_AND_LOOK_BACK / DOUBLE_TAKE]
   *FOLLOW_01   "Why are you following me?"
   *FOLLOW_02   "I literally just moved."
    FOLLOW_03   "Okay, that's... not funny."
    FOLLOW_04   "Are you doing this on purpose?"
    FOLLOW_05   "Seriously?"

6. KEEPS HAPPENING (OnRepeatedBoundaryViolation)  [ANNOYED_REACTION]
   *REPEAT_01   "I've asked you nicely."
    REPEAT_02   "Why do you keep doing that?"
    REPEAT_03   "What is WRONG with you?"
    REPEAT_04   "Every time. Every single time."

7. HE SNAPS (OnWarning)  — the only LOUD section   [NERVOUS_WARNING / STEP_BACK_FAST]
   *WARN_01     "Get away from me!"               LOUD, voice cracks a bit
   *WARN_02     "Back OFF!"                       LOUD
    WARN_03     "I said leave me ALONE!"          LOUD
    WARN_04     "Stop it! Just stop!"             LOUD, more scared than angry
    WARN_05     "Don't touch me!"                 LOUD

8. YOU BACKED OFF (OnPlayerBackedOff)       [DEEP_BREATH / LOOK_BACK_AT_PLAYER]
   *BACKOFF_01  "...Thanks."
    BACKOFF_02  "Okay. Thank you."
    BACKOFF_03  "Sorry. I just don't like people that close."
    BACKOFF_04  (long relieved exhale)

9. CALMS DOWN (OnRecovered)                 [IDLE_NEUTRAL_A / RUB_HANDS]
    RECOVER_01  "Okay. Fine. It's fine."          (to himself)
    RECOVER_02  "Breathe, Elliot."                (whisper)
    RECOVER_03  "Weird day."
    RECOVER_04  (small sigh)

10. BUMPED / HIT (collision, impact)        [BUMP_RECOIL_* / SHOULDER_IMPACT_* / STUMBLE_BACK]
   *BUMP_01     "Oh— sorry! Sorry."               (apologises even though YOU hit HIM)
   *BUMP_02     "Ow!"
   *HIT_01      "What was that for?!"
    HIT_02      "Did you just— did you just hit me?"
    HIT_03      "Ow. Okay. Ow."
    BUMP_03     "My bad— wait, that was you."
